OpenEHDS is an enabler: it builds reference components and architecture patterns for EHDS secondary use.

## What we build

* permit-aware dataset assembly engines
* policy enforcement modules for access and purpose constraints
* connectors across data holders, access bodies, and secure environments
* audit, logging, lineage, and TTL lifecycle components

## What we do not do

* we do not act as a health data access body
* we do not host or monetize medical data
* we do not operate secure processing environments

This role separation builds trust and improves component reusability across national implementations.
