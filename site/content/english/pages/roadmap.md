The roadmap translates OpenEHDS mission into concrete delivery milestones.

1. establish transparent governance and open contribution workflows for a neutral foundation model
2. publish the first permit-aware dataset assembly and policy enforcement reference components
3. release integration patterns for secure processing environments, including audit, lineage, TTL, and controlled deletion controls
4. expand interoperability mappings aligned with EHDS and FHIR, with adaptation guidance for national implementations
5. publish education and implementation resources that close EHDS knowledge gaps across stakeholders

The sequencing reflects one principle: make EHDS implementation practical, auditable, and reusable instead of fragmented and opaque.
