Bogusław Budziński, EMBA

Governmental Relations & Public Affairs Expert
Foundation Council Member

An experienced executive and healthcare market expert with a long-standing career in Poland and internationally. He launched and managed the Polish subsidiaries of international pharmaceutical companies, including Laboratories Stiefel (now Glaxo Wellcome) and Laboratoires Dolisos (Pierre Fabre Group, now Boiron). He co-founded and formerly held shares in companies operating in clinical drug research and extracorporeal therapeutic blood purification. He previously served as management board representative and partner at DOM LEKARSKI S.A., and as president of the Innovative Medicine Association INKLAMED. He currently serves as Poland’s national coordinator for the HOPE Exchange programme for healthcare managers, run by the Polish Hospital Federation and the European Hospital and Healthcare Federation.

Co-founder

Tomasz Kulbacki

Entrepreneur and business development leader with more than 15 years of experience across technology, healthcare and the public sector. He specialises in strategic partnerships and interoperable digital ecosystems.

He is CEO of Detable and owner of IT Design. His earlier work includes roles at Britenet, Philips Lighting Poland and Simple S.A.

At OpenEHDS, Tomasz focuses on practical EHDS infrastructure, open-source collaboration, regulatory readiness and systemic health data exchange.

Co-founder

Ania Adamiak

Digital transformation adviser and strategist with more than 15 years of experience across technology, healthcare and innovation. She is co-founder and CEO of EENGINE Software House.

Her background combines technology and business with academic training in evolutionary biology and an MBA.

At OpenEHDS, Ania translates regulation and interoperability into practical, open and implementation-ready infrastructure approaches.
